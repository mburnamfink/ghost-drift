# Vehicle controls with a chase camera, not twin-stick

The Ship steers like a vehicle: throttle on a trigger, turn with the left stick, guns fixed along the Heading, camera behind and above the Ship. Aiming independently of travel is earned through Powerslide, which bleeds speed, the Ship's only defense.

A twin-stick shooter (move with one stick, aim with the other, top-down camera) is the obvious alternative for an arena shooter and was rejected: free aiming removes the core tension between moving fast and pointing guns at things, and a top-down view loses the Battlezone/Wipeout sense of speed through terrain.

## Consequences

Enemy and level design assume the player mostly shoots forward. Threats from behind and the sides are answered by turning, sliding or Bursting, not by aiming.
